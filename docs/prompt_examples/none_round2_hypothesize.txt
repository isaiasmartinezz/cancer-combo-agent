## Literature
How to read the literature: how much has been published about a drug reflects how much it has been studied, not how well it combines. Research compounds with few papers are as likely to score high in this screen as famous drugs. Papers describe other cells and conditions; when this lab's results disagree with a paper, go with the results. Class evidence is about other members of a drug's class, not the compound itself.

...The synthesized CDDP-BEZ nanoparticles (NPs) possess sensitive pH-responsiveness, facilitating the delivery of both drugs to cancer cells.
